01 Millimeter Visuals

The details are the story.

Millimeter Visuals is a Boston photography and video company, led by its founder and built on one belief: the smallest choices decide how a moment is remembered.

A glance held a beat longer. Light across a hand. The quiet before a toast. Every portrait, wedding, event and brand project is built around those small things, so the finished work feels natural, never staged. It’s why the ruler is part of the brand: slow down, measure what matters, and get it exactly right.

02 Founder & Creative Director

Oliver

Photographer & Media Arts

Oliver is a photographer and Media Arts graduate based in Boston, MA. He has documented solemn events and non-profit initiatives, with his work featured across local publications and digital platforms.

As the Founder and Creative Director of Millimeter Visuals, Oliver specializes in portraits, weddings, events, and visual media tailored for growing brands and individuals.

When he’s not behind the camera or in the editing room, Oliver focuses on expanding his creative repertoire and mastering new visual technologies.
